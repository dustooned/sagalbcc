🎲 SAGA Manual

A live 3D playtest table for your group. Runs in the browser on PCs, tablets and phones.

Quick start

  1. Open SAGA, type your name and the class password (ask your instructor).
  2. Host creates a room. Everyone else joins with the room code or the invite link.
  3. Load your kit from Kit Forge: drag the .kittable.json onto the table. A LOL, FIGHT TIEM! deck from Card Forge works too: drag its .lftdeck.json (Send to playtest).
  4. Play. Everyone sees every move live.
Tap ✕ (top right) to hide all panels for a clear view; ☰ brings them back. The 🧰 Table tools panel folds shut from its header.

Adding things to the table

📦 Load kit…A .kittable.json from Kit Forge's Send to Table or Export ZIP, or a .lftdeck.json from Card Forge's Send to playtest.
🖼️ Add image…Any PNG/JPG/WebP becomes a piece. Fastest way to test one idea. A big photo (over 2048 px or 1.5 MB; 4096 px for table and background images) is shrunk first so it loads fast for everyone — SAGA asks, and you can keep the original if it's under 8 MB.
🧊 Add 3D model…A .glb, .obj or .stl. See File formatsAnimated3D models.
🎨 Table look → BackgroundSpace, nebula and gradient presets, your own gradient or image, plus a Wave or Kaleidoscope effect. Atmosphere adds fog, desert dust, rain, snow or embers with a strength slider. Everyone sees the same look.
Markers & notes+1, −1, DMG, STATUS, custom labels, notes, and dice.
Drag & dropDrop any of the files above anywhere on the page.

Clear table is for the host and co-hosts only (see Pointers & people).

Pointers & people

Pointers and pings

Everyone sees where everyone else is pointing: a ring in that player's colour follows their mouse over the felt and fades out about two seconds after they stop moving. You never see your own ring.

Alt+clickPing: a pulse on the spot you click, so you can say "this one" without describing it. Works on top of a piece too — it pings instead of picking the piece up.
PStop sharing your pointer (press again to share it). Only affects you.

Pointers come from a mouse or pen; on a phone or tablet your finger isn't broadcast. Pointers and pings aren't part of the table: they are never undone, saved or written to the log.

Watching without a seat

A table seats 8. Anyone can also join as a spectator — tick 👁 Watch without taking a seat before joining. Spectators see everything live and can point and ping, but they can't move, add or change anything, and they don't use up a seat, so an instructor can watch a full table. Up to 8 people can watch at once. They're listed under the party as 👁 WATCHING.

Host, co-hosts and bans

The person who opens a table is the host (👑). If the host leaves, the next seated player takes over — a spectator never becomes host.

☆ / ⭐Host only: make a seated player a co-host. A co-host can remove and ban players and clear the table, the same as the host. Click the ⭐ again to take it back — it's immediate. Co-hosts show a ⭐ next to their name.
✕Host or co-host: remove a player. They can join again.
🚫Host or co-host: remove a player and stop that name from rejoining this table. Banned names are listed under the party with an Unban button.
A ban is by name, so somebody determined can rejoin under a different name. It's a classroom convenience behind the class password, not security. Nobody can remove or ban the host. Bans and co-hosts last as long as the table is open: undo never brings a banned player back, and saved tables don't include bans.

Saving the table

💾 Save table (in 🧰 Table tools) downloads a .sagatable.json file holding everything on the table: every piece with its position, size, rotation, face and lock, every marker and note, the point counters, and the table look. Nothing is uploaded — the file lands in your own Downloads folder.

Put it back later with 📂 Load table…, or by dropping the file onto the page. Loading replaces what's on the table, so it asks first; Ctrl+Z restores the previous table if you picked the wrong file.

Pictures and 3D models are referenced by the id this server gave them, so a save file can never pull in a picture from somewhere else. If those uploaded files are gone — a redeploy that lost the uploads folder, say — the pieces come back without their artwork, though everything else about them does.

The table also saves itself. Every couple of minutes the server writes the table to its own disk. If a session ends without anyone saving — a crash, a redeploy, a laptop closing — then the next person to open a table gets a bar at the top of the screen: "Saved table SAGA-82K — Sat 4:12 PM, 23 pieces." Press Restore and it is back; Ctrl+Z undoes the restore if you did not want it.

Between autosaves and manual saves the table survives a restart, but not instantly — the last couple of minutes before a crash are gone. Save by hand if you are about to close the laptop mid-playtest.

Grid & naming pieces

🧰 Table tools → GRID puts a 1″, 2″ or 4″ grid on the felt. With it on, a piece you drop clicks to the nearest line — and a piece dropped near a stack still snaps onto the stack instead. The grid is shared like the felt, and it is saved with the table.

Right-click a piece → ✏️ Rename… changes what the log and the menu call it; 📝 Note on the piece… adds a description that appears under the name in that same menu. Neither is a rule — they're labels your party can read.

A pinned piece shows a small padlock and a grey ring. Press K or use the menu to free it.

Dice & tokens

Dice: D6 (numbers or dots), D4, D8, D10, D12, D20. Everyone sees the same result.

Click / tapRoll in place.
ShakeHold it and wiggle back and forth, then let go — it rolls.
Flick / throwDrag fast and release. It slides, tumbles and bounces off the rim.

DMG, STATUS and custom tokens can be thrown too — they flip like a coin.

Animated pieces (GIF, WebM, MP4)

Drop a GIF or a short clip on the table (or 🖼️ Add image…) and it plays on the piece for everyone, looping, with no sound.

FormatLimitsTransparency
GIF8 MB · 300 frames · 20 s · 1024 pxYes, everywhere
WebM (VP9)10 MB · 10 s · 1280 pxYes on Chrome, Edge, Firefox. iPhone/iPad can’t show transparent WebM.
MP4 (H.264)10 MB · 10 s · 1280 pxNo — MP4 has no transparency

Clear areas of a token show the table through them. If a file is too long or too big, SAGA tells you exactly what to trim — ezgif.com can resize, cut and convert GIFs and clips for free.

3D models from Blender

Easiest: any .obj or .stl works as-is — it just shows in one plain color.

Textured OBJ: select the .obj, its .mtl and all its image files together (or drop one .zip of the folder). SAGA matches the images by file name — even when the .mtl points to a folder on someone else's computer — and bakes them into the model. Textures bigger than 2048 px are shrunk unless you say no; images without see-through parts are stored as JPEG to keep the model small. If an image is missing, SAGA names it and asks whether to add the model without it.

For the best look (shine, metal, lighting detail), export .glb with the settings below.

Fastest: the SAGA Blender add-on

Bakes procedural materials, color ramps and textures into one small .glb with one button — no settings to get right.

  1. Download saga_bake_addon.py.
  2. Blender: Edit → Preferences → Add-ons → ⌄ → Install from Disk…, pick the file, tick SAGA Bake & Export.
  3. Select your model, press N, open the SAGA tab, click Bake & Export.
  4. Drop the .glb (saved next to your .blend) onto the table.

Can't install add-ons on a school computer? Use saga_bake.py instead: Scripting tab → Text → Open → ▶ Run.

Or export by hand

In SAGA, the ⓘ next to 🧊 Add 3D model… shows these settings too.

Before exporting

  1. Select only your model (no lights or camera).
  2. Press Ctrl+A → All Transforms.
  3. Check the triangle count: Overlays → Statistics.

Export: File → Export → glTF 2.0

Troubleshooting

Stuck on loading / "handshake"Reload the page. Check you're online.
"Someone is moving that"Another player is holding it. Wait a second.
Table disappearedThe room closes after 30 minutes with nobody in it, or when the server updates. Reload your kit.
Can't see the table on a phoneTap ✕ to hide panels.
